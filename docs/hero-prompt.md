# Final hero generation brief

Tool: built-in image generation. Identity reference: `inspiration/me.webp`.

Create ONE square website hero illustration in MINIMALIST FLAT VECTOR STYLE. Not a photograph, not painterly, not detailed anime. Very simple clean geometric shapes, solid flat color fills, sparse uniform outlines, maximum 2 tones per object, no textures, NO grain, no tiny details, no realistic rendering, no elaborate shading. Identity reference photo only informs short black hair, rectangular dark glasses, moustache and short beard, brown skin. Draw a simple cartoon caricature of this man in a plain coffee brown T-shirt seated on the RIGHT, facing LEFT toward his computer, seen in side/back profile, looking at screen and working, NOT at viewer. Minimal wooden desk across lower middle, monitor on LEFT with just a few simple game landscape shapes, plain keyboard, one plant with 6 big green leaves, simple lamp, two hanging warm yellow bulbs. Dark espresso solid background exactly #211813. Simple dark blue rectangle window, no shelves or clutter. Keep scene airy with generous negative space around objects. Tight palette dark espresso, walnut, caramel, muted green, warm yellow, small blue accent screen/window. At bottom left on the desk place a small plain cream coffee mug. Its OPEN RIM CENTER must be approximately x=30% and y=69% of full square canvas. Leave EMPTY DARK SPACE directly above cup for separate animated steam to be added in website. ABSOLUTELY NO STEAM, NO VAPOR, NO SMOKE anywhere in this image. Crisp simple vector-style artwork, clean flat shapes, restrained details, charming minimal caricature. Entire illustration must feel like it could be constructed from 40-60 simple SVG paths. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. The cup's steam will be animated externally; do NOT draw steam.

Output integrated at `public/images/workstation-640.webp` and `public/images/workstation-960.webp`. The output mug rim is approximately x=20.5%, y=69.5%; the separate steam overlay follows the actual image position, not the requested position.
